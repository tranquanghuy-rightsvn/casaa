    Privacy policy

    Applies to the website 3d-printedfacade.com · Updated 01/11/2026

    This policy explains how Casa+ (C.A.S Services and Education Joint Stock Company, “we”) collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit the website 3d-printedfacade.com, create an account or send a consultation request.

    1. Information we collect

    When you create an account with email

    • Your name, email address and password. Passwords are hashed one-way before storage; we cannot see your original password.
    • Your phone number, if you add it on the account page.

    When you sign in with Google

    • We only receive from Google: your name, email address (verified by Google), profile picture and Google account ID.
    • We do not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other data in your Google account, and never know your Google password.

    When you send a consultation request

    • Your need (individual or business), name, phone number, email and the content you enter in the contact form.

    Technical information

    • When you visit the website, our servers automatically record some technical information such as IP address, browser type and time of access, for operation, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.
    • The website uses Google Analytics to measure visits: pages viewed, time on page, traffic source, device type and approximate region. This data is used to understand and improve the website, not to identify individuals.
    • Website do not use advertising tools.

    2. How we use information

    • Create and maintain your account and authenticate you when you sign in.
    • Contact you for consultation and quotes, and deliver the design and production services you request.
    • Protect accounts and systems: limit failed sign-in attempts and detect unusual access.
    • Comply with legal obligations.

    We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

    3. Cookie

    The website uses two types of cookies:

    Essential cookies

    • Session cookie (casa_session): keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. This cookie cannot be read by JavaScript and is only sent over secure HTTPS connections.
    • Google sign-in cookie (casa_oauth): lasts at most 10 minutes while you sign in with Google, to prevent forged requests.

    Analytics cookies

    • Cookie Google Analytics (_ga, _ga_*): distinguish visits for statistics, stored for up to 2 years. You can block them in your browser settings or with the add-on Google Analytics Opt-out.

    You can delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; if you delete the session cookie, you will need to sign in again.

    4. Sharing with service providers

    To run the website we use several infrastructure services. These providers only process data as needed to provide their services to us:

    • Cloudflare: hosting the website and account database, protection against attacks.
    • Google: authentication when you choose Google sign-in; visit statistics (Google Analytics); fonts (Google Fonts).
    • jsDelivr: providing the page animation library.

    We only disclose information to competent authorities upon a lawful request under applicable law.

    5. Retention and security

    • Account information is kept while your account is active and deleted when you request account deletion.
    • Consultation requests are kept as long as needed for consultation, contract performance and record-keeping requirements.
    • All connections to the website are encrypted with HTTPS; passwords are hashed one-way; internal access to data is limited to responsible staff.

    No system is completely secure. If we detect an incident affecting your data, we will notify you and handle it in accordance with the law.

    6. Your rights

    • View and edit your name and phone number on the account page.
    • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Request deletion of your account and related data, or withdraw your consent to data processing.
    • Unlink Google: you can remove Casa+'s access on your Google account page (myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps).

    To exercise these rights, email us from your registered address. We will reply within 7 working days.

    7. Children

    The website is not intended for children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and know that a child has given us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

    8. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy when we change how the website operates or when the law changes. The update date is shown at the top of the page; for significant changes we will post a notice on the website.
