    About us

    Casa+ was founded on 20.08.2021 by architect Hoàng Thiện as a brand of C.A.S Services & Education JSC (C.A.S JSC). We work as a consultancy providing technology-driven creative solutions, focused on architecture, interiors, industrial design, spatial services and design education.

    Casa+ focuses on researching, designing and delivering complex solutions that combine design thinking, digital technology and real making. We accompany clients from strategic direction and solution development through to application and operation, creating lasting, practical value.

    Building a creative ecosystem with Vietnamese identity, Casa+ connects people, technology and knowledge — raising the design and creative capacity of Vietnamese practitioners in an international context.

    Vision

    To become the leading Vietnamese creative ecosystem applying digital technology to architecture and materials — where traditional ceramics continue through algorithms and 3D printers.

    Mission

    To connect people, technology and knowledge so design ideas move from screen to workshop to real buildings, while training a generation of architects fluent in parametric design.

    Core values

    Creativity grounded in research. Precision in every fabrication parameter. Sustainability in materials and method. Respect for Vietnamese identity in every product.
