    Process

    Story → Research → Design → Production → Installation

    1. Story
    2. Research
    3. Design
    4. Production
    5. Installation

    Step 1

    Story

    Every project begins with a story: the people who live in it, the climate it stands in, and the memory the client wants to keep. We sit with the client and the architect to hear all of that before we talk about materials.

    From that conversation Casa+ records the core idea, budget, timeline and technical constraints — the foundation for all four steps that follow.

    View this step in detail →

    Step 2

    Research

    Đông xưởng — the R&D centre — studies computational design algorithms, analyses daylight and natural ventilation, and programs optimal toolpaths for 3D-printed ceramic.

    The result is a verifiable set of geometric parameters — porosity, wall thickness, sun angle — enough to move into design without guesswork.

    View this step in detail →

    Step 3

    Design

    The Nam xưởng design studio develops the aesthetic idea and collection forms: patterns, glaze colours, module sizes and how the tiles join across a wall.

    Drawings are checked against the real space through visualisations and small test samples for the client to approve before production.

    View this step in detail →

    Step 4

    Production

    Tây xưởng produces the physical prototype on ceramic 3D printers, firing and glazing to the design file. A 1:1 mock-up checks colour, surface and fixing before the full run.

    Once the sample is approved, the workshop produces and inspects each batch to keep consistency across deliveries.

    View this step in detail →

    Step 5

    Installation

    The installation team builds on site from shop drawings, with framing and mortar suited to each position: interior walls, exterior facades or breeze screens.

    On completion Casa+ hands over maintenance documentation so the ceramic keeps its beauty over time.

    View this step in detail →
