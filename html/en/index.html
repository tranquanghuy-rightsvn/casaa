    Process

    Five steps from the story of a project to the installed tile. Choose a step to read the details.

    1. Story Listening to the client, the architect and the site to shape the project’s idea.
    2. Research Đông xưởng – R&D centre: parametric design algorithms for 3D-printed ceramics.
    3. Design Nam xưởng design studio: developing the aesthetic idea and collection forms.
    4. Production Tây xưởng: prototyping on ceramic 3D printers, then production to order.
    5. Installation Installation and finishing on site to the approved technical documents.
