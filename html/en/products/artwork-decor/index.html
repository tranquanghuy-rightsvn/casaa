    ARTWORK & DECOR

    01

    Researching ideas & artistic language

    The creative process begins by studying sources of artistic, cultural, natural and architectural inspiration. Abstract ideas are turned into a language of form, line and spatial structure, giving each work its own identity. C.A.S treats clay as a medium of artistic expression, combining traditional creative thinking with digital forming technology.

    02

    Form development & digital modelling

    Studying forming methods and developing product geometry through 3D models. Using Rhino, Grasshopper and computational design to control shape, thickness, hollow structure, patterns and complex details, while optimising the design for clay 3D printing.

    03

    3D printing & prototyping

    Setting up the print process around material properties and product geometry: preparing clay, treating the mix, setting machine parameters, designing the print-head path and controlling material extrusion. Printing samples, assessing forming errors and adjusting parameters before production.

    04

    Post-processing & finished ceramic production

    Building a finishing process covering surface treatment, controlled drying, fault repair, firing and post-firing finishing. Checking shrinkage, deformation, cracking, surface quality and consistency between products to ensure stable production.
