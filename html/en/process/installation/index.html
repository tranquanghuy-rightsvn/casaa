    01

    304 stainless-steel support frame

    The frame and fixings are engineered for wind load and site conditions. 304 stainless resists corrosion in coastal and urban environments.

    02

    Aligning the joints

    Modules are numbered and installed in the designed sequence. Casa⁺ works with the contractor to align level, vertical joints and the flatness of the whole wall.

    03

    Inspection & handover

    After installation, the work is checked for fixing strength, drainage and surface cleanliness. Casa⁺ hands over with maintenance guidance so the material keeps its colour and structure.
